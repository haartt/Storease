# 📖 Storease

### Turn your photos into stories — one step at a time.

Storease turns your spare moments into creative ones. Pick a photo — one you just took on a walk, on the train, or anywhere in between — and start building a short story around it.

The app guides you step by step: you write a line, then choose from AI-generated continuations to keep the story going. Each choice shapes what comes next, until you end up with a complete short story attached to a picture that means something to you.

No blank page, no pressure. Just a photo, a few taps, and a story that's yours.

---

## How it works

1. **Pick a photo** from your library or take one in the moment
2. **Write your opening** — just a sentence is enough
3. **Choose a continuation** from AI-suggested options at each step
4. **Repeat** until your story feels complete
5. **Keep it** — the story lives alongside your photo

---

## Built with

- **SwiftUI** for the interface
- **Apple FoundationModels** for on-device AI story generation — private, fast, no internet required
